Our latest dinner · Denver, October 1, 2026

Twenty seats. Twenty-six guests. A waitlist.

We opened our Denver dinner at Coohills with drinks on the patio at golden hour, then moved inside to four tables. Founders came out of the Colorado School of Mines and local accelerators, alongside exited founders, operators and investors.

Our sponsors, Wells Fargo and FinStrat Management, were part of the evening, not just on the menu. Each was seated with the guests most relevant to them, had a moment to speak over dinner, and left with the full guest list and a set of introductions to follow up on.

People stayed long after dessert. One guest told us she'd planned to drop in for an hour and was still there four hours later.

More than a logo on a table

Most event sponsorships buy you a banner and a hope. Ours are built so that every sponsor leaves with real relationships.

Every seat is intentional

We don't do open seating. We plan every table by hand, seed conversations before the first course, and rotate seats between courses so the networking stays fresh all night. The room is electric because it's designed to be.

The guest list, before the night

Sponsors get the full guest list with contact details ahead of the event, so you arrive knowing who you want to meet and why. We'll also seat you next to them.

Featured before and after

Our pre-event email introduces you to every guest. Our post-event recap thanks you by name, alongside the photos, quotes and every guest's LinkedIn. Your brand travels well beyond the room.

Your brand on the night

Your logo on the printed menus and our custom notebooks at every place setting, plus a moment to speak to the room over dinner. Understated, never salesy.

Photo and video, included

We hire a professional photographer and videographer for every event. You get the photos, guest testimonials and highlight edits to use in your own channels.

Access to our deal flow

The founders in our rooms come from the accelerators and venture studios we have worked alongside for years. Sponsors meet them early, well before the rest of the market does.
