We'll be there

Venture Builders at the Whitefish Security Summit

We'll be in Whitefish, Montana for the second annual Whitefish Security Summit, where the 2027 focus is autonomous warfare meeting artificial intelligence. If you're building, funding or buying defense and dual-use technology, we'd like to meet you there.

Book time with us in WhitefishAbout the summit
When
February 24 to 26, 2027
Where
Whitefish, MontanaFlathead Valley
Summit focus
Autonomous warfare meets artificial intelligence
Who's going
The Venture Builders Fund team

Why we're going

Defense founders need investors and buyers in the same room

Founders building for national security face a hard path: long sales cycles, a small set of buyers and investors who don't always know the space. The Whitefish Security Summit brings practitioners, technologists and funders together for three days, and we're going to meet the founders in the middle of it.

We're also looking at hosting a small gathering for defense founders and investors around the summit. If you'd like to be part of it, as a guest or a sponsor, get in touch.

Defense and dual-use founders

Especially those coming out of accelerators and looking for their first large customer.

Government innovation funders

Programs that invest in and buy from early-stage defense companies, in the US and allied countries.

Investors

Funds and angels active in national security and dual-use technology.

Accelerator and ecosystem leaders

Programs supporting defense founders who want to compare notes.

The Whitefish Security Summit is run in partnership with the Lobo Institute. Venture Builders Fund is attending and is not an organizer of the summit.
