The program

Nine months from baseline to investor showcase

A blended format built for founders who are running active companies: three full-day in-person gatherings, with virtual sessions and self-paced work in between.

01

Foundation (Months 1 to 2)

An in-person kickoff forms the cohort and sets each founder's baseline. Founders complete a diagnostic of how their business operates and begin building their operational playbook.

02

Build (Months 3 to 5)

Monthly leadership and culture sessions, self-paced modules on core business systems, and a mid-program in-person intensive. One-on-one capital strategy conversations begin.

03

Capital Readiness (Months 6 to 8)

Peer consultation, delegation and team design, investor-grade financials and a personal leadership statement. Investor office hours run alongside, and warm introductions begin.

04

Showcase (Month 9)

Founders complete pitch coaching and present to a curated audience of investors at a full-day closing event. A final assessment shows measurable growth against the baseline.
