Venture Builders Retreat · October 2026

Ireland and Italy

A week built for the people building the next generation of accelerators, venture studios, and innovation hubs, starting in Limerick at Futura Innovation Conference, moving on to Milan for CES Unveiled, and finishing at H-Farm in Venice, with a Saturday visit to the Biennale.

This is an invitation to build relationships, not just fill an agenda. Along the way, there's time and space for the conversations that matter most.

Limerick, Ireland · Oct 19–20
Milan, Italy · Oct 21–22
Venice, Italy · Oct 23–24

The stops

One week, two countries, three stops

01

Futura Innovation Conference

Limerick, Ireland October 19–20

A citywide innovation gathering bringing together founders, investors, researchers, and builders across Ireland, Europe, and beyond. Venture Builders Retreat guests will be there October 19–20, joining as part of the broader Futura community.

There's a pub in Limerick with our name on it! In collaboration with Funded House, Venture Builders Retreat guests will have a home base while at Futura: Funded House at Jerry Flannery's Bar, 20 Catherine St, Limerick. Less programming, more of the people you'll want to know. Open to you throughout the conference.

Venture builder show-and-tell: Monday, October 19, in the afternoon (exact time to be confirmed).

02

CES Unveiled by CTA

Milan, Italy October 21

A single-day showcase of what's next in technology and venture, hosted by CTA at Allianz MiCo on Wednesday, October 21, from noon to 7 PM, with a cocktail reception from 5 to 7 PM. Venture Builders Retreat guests get access and a VIP tour of select startups with Brian Comiskey, CTA's futurist.

Thursday, October 22, 6:00 PM: cocktails at Le Village by CA, the Crédit Agricole startup accelerator at Corso di Porta Romana 61, Milan, hosted by Business France.

03

H-Farm

Venice and Treviso, Italy October 23–24

A visit to H-Farm, one of Europe's leading venture builders, set on a campus in the Venice countryside. On Friday, October 23, we arrive by train for lunch with the entrepreneurship fellows, a campus tour, and a fireside conversation about US startups and investing led by Tim O'Connell, followed by drinks with the group and the H-Farm team. On Saturday, October 24, an optional visit to the Venice Biennale. This stop is being planned for a small group, with details to follow directly to anyone joining.

Logistics

Getting there and where we're staying

Futura runs across Limerick city centre, with venues within walking distance of each other. CES Unveiled is a single day in Milan at Allianz MiCo. Below are the recommended logistics for each leg, from Limerick to Milan to H-Farm in Venice. Please book your own travel and accommodation and do what works best for your schedule.
