Getting there and where we're staying

Futura runs across Limerick city centre, with venues within walking distance of each other. CES Unveiled is a single day in Milan at Allianz MiCo. If you're joining both, here's how the group is planning the leg between them, and where we're staying.

Confirmed

Limerick to Milan

The group is planning to fly Aer Lingus EI432, departing Dublin early morning and landing at Milan Linate. Linate is the closest airport to Allianz MiCo, about 15 to 20 minutes away, which matters more than an earlier departure time from a farther airport. This is the only daily direct-adjacent option on this route, so we'd recommend booking it with a flexible fare, and booking early.

Getting around

Limerick to Dublin Airport: about 2 hours by car. A private car or shuttle is the recommended option given the timing, especially for anyone traveling straight from Futura's final evening.

Milan Linate to Allianz MiCo: about 15 to 25 minutes by taxi or rideshare. No train needed for this leg given the short distance.

Hotel names above are being finalized. The Milan property and the Dublin airport hotel pick will be confirmed and added here with booking links shortly.
